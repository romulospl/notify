# Support

Dione and Rômulo are responsible for support, through the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/romulospl/stellytics/issues), as RFP section 3.4 allows. Commitment: first human response within **5 business days** and resolution within **10 business days** of the issue being opened.

- Do not post credentials, personal data or vulnerability details in issues. Vulnerabilities go through the private channel and deadlines in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
- Resolution means the fix is confirmed, or a reasoned answer concludes the request, and the requester is told; closing an issue without saying so does not count.
